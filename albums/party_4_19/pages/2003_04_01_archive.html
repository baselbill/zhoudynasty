Weekend Summary

>Friday
I went to a white people's wedding with Lisa. One of Lisa's good friends was getting married and she's in the ceremony so she needed a date for the occasion. It was pretty cool. The ceremony was kind of weird cuz it was all church like and I didn't know what the heck they were singing, I just felt out of place. Oh well at least no one pointed and laughed at me. The reception was pretty cool, we had some good food and champagne and wine. Then the white peoples started dancing to polka and swing, I thought that was interesting. I tried swing dancing a little bit but didn't have enough alcohol in my system to get down and polka.
After we got back from the wedding we went directly to Ryan's house party all dressed up. It was pretty fun, the asians were dancing and drinking, the whites were watching the Wilds hockey game and drinking..hehe.

>Saturday
This is the day I finally got insurance activated again on my BMW. Thank god cuz it was a nice day out. I went to CASA's picnic, it was a great time. The board members arranged for an ambush with waterballoons and water guns on all the other members. It turned out to be one big ass water fight. A lot of people just got totally soaked, I think Josh got it all on his camcorder. We also played football and softball. Check out the pics and you'll see brave Dewitt retrieve the ball I hit onto the roof. But then when he was gonna come down he realized that he didn't have enough energy so the rest of CASA had to help him down.
After I got off work Saturday I went to Ben's party at his apartment. I brought Wu and his friend Preston. This was the first time that I ever hung out with Wu but he's a pretty cool guy, he can drink a lot. The party was great except it was too nice outside so a lot of people ended up sitting on the grass and smoking. I got home at some time, I forgot cuz I was drunk.
