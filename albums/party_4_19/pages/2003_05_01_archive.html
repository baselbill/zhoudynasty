Sunday, May 25, 2003

Non-stop Partying
Wow I think I am almost partied out. I say almost because today is David's birthday and it's going to be the last party for me for a while. All this excitement does no good for me and my wallet.
Thursday night was Tasha's birthday party at Townsquare Garden. It was a pretty good party but I think it could've been better if the bar had more drinks and if her party didn't have to compete with the Quest that night. Overall it was a lot of fun and I randomly met another girl that already knew my dad before she knew me. My dad was also her instructor at CADT.
Friday night we went out to Harvey's for Saba's birthday last week. Originally we were going to go to Ocean Club after 10 because that's when Harveys ends their happy hour. But by that time everyone was having a lot of fun and decided to stay at Harvey's. Kenneth came too, finally, after years of begging him to hang out and party with me.
Last night Linh threw a big asian party at her house because her parents were out of town. I kept myself under control because the past two nights were already a lot for me. However other people were pretty out there, dancing and getting drunk. Great party but too bad she had to spend all day today cleaning up.
Today is a good day. All I've done today was lay on my deck and absorbed sun. And thought about things. Sigh, I wish I could see my future more clearly.

Monday, May 19, 2003

The pain and suffering is over
"So what are you gonna do now?"
"I donno, pick my nose?"
...yeah baby, summer is here.

Yes I'm done with another year of college, wow it seemed really fast. Seems like just last year I was a freshman, now I am going to be a senior. This is getting scarry, getting closer and closer to graduation and not being able to see past that. Well for now I'm gonna think that far, I'm just gonna concentrate on the parties for this week...hehe. As all of you may or may not know, there's gonna be a big birthday bash that Tasha is throwing on Thursday. If you don't know who she is, she is that tall asian girl...the one taller than me. Yeah she's turning 21 and she's throwing a big party at Townsquare with "Gordon". Gordon is that asian guy that all other asians know as Gordon the guy who throws those asian parties. So anyways, you all better be there this Thursday or you're all gonna miss out. Then Friday there's a group of us going to downtown for Saba's 21st birthday last week. We're gonna hit Harvey's for their $2 specials then go to Ocean Club afterwards, 21+ please. Saturday my friend Linh is throwing a party at her house in Woodbury because her parents are out of town. Sunday is my David's birthday and we're gonna to the Quest for that big asian event where that import model is gonna be making an appearance at. After that I think he wants to go see some naked chicks. So yeah if any of these events entice you, give me a call.

Oh so what am I really gonna do for the summer? Take EE3025 Statistical Methods for EE. And then maybe find a job so I don't have to work at Peking Way. And party. Oh yeah, play a hell of a lot of Counter-Strike. Damn I need that CS! But first I need to get cable modem at home.

Studying is futile
