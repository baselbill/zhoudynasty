Wednesday, June 11, 2003

Billy's Nightmare!
Omg last night I didn't sleep very well at all. I have this incredible nightmare that made me feel like my heart was about to jump out of my chest!
I don't know if you've heard of my UFO story yet but once I saw this ufo that looked like a big bright moon. Anyways, last night I dreampt that I was at a diner with some friends after a day of waterskiing. Then I saw out of the window the UFO that I had seen before in real life. So I pointed it out to someone and I said "Look! There's that ufo I had seen before, except this time it looks even bigger." Then this thing started to move, real fast across the horizon. It seemed like it knew that we had spotted the irregularities. I was like "Holy shit get inside!" Then this UFO started to circle around the dinner emitting some kind of light. Everyone was freaking out and I saying to myself "Am I dreaming or is this UFO gonna get us?" It seemed so real at the time, the fear, the table I was hiding under all seemed so real, it just freaked me out even more. Then this UFO stopped and landed infront of the diner in a saucer-like manner and started emitting some sort of light pulses at everyone. This was the worse part of the dream. Everyone was scared and thought they were going to die or get abducted. I knew this was my last chance to do anything before I die. So I ran out to the landed UFO and ripped a chunk out of it. Then I ate it.
And it tasted good! It tasted like this Chinese bread I used to eat. Crispy outside and really soft of the inside and kind of sweet. Then I just helped myself to another server of the UFO bread and ate some more. Then my mom came out and started helping me to eat this tasty UFO. Before you know it, the UFO was defeated and everyone was saved! Then I woke up and I was really hungry.
