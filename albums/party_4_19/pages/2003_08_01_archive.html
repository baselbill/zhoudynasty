Wrenching
I worked on my bike today with my friend Sheldon. I drove over the Woodbury and we installed Yoyodyne frame sliders on my bike! It was a lot of fun working with him and it was the first time meeting him.
The sliders look real nice and fit perfectly. We had to cut a hole 2 inches in diameter on both sides of the fairing, that was scary! Well now that I have the sliders on, if my bike tips over again, it won't damage my fairings. Too bad I couldn't put them in yesterday. =(

Friday, August 29, 2003

Sad things T_T
I went for a little ride yesterday with Lisa. Everything was fine and we were cruising at slow speeds. Then we got to an enclosed road and decided to trade bikes and try each other's ouch. Well I got on hers and went about 30 feet, then I hear CRACK! I turned around and she tipped my bike over! The bike wasn't even moving yet and she tried to balance it but couldn't and the whole thing fell over. It got scratched up some more. Well at least I can go buy that touch-up paint now. -_-

Last night my friend Sara came to visit my new apartment and we decided to go grab a bite to eat. We hopped on our motorcycles and decided to take the long way there, go ride around campus first. We got excited and started riding a little faster. But then when we got to this one bridge I said "We gotta becareful cuz there are a lot of cops on this bridge." And we still raced over that thing. I guess she just couldn't let me go faster than her. I started to do a lean and that's when I noticed the flashing lights. F(*@&#*(@&#(@#*&(@&! We got pulled over, Sara was terrified cuz she had a reckless driving citation last month and was sure she's not gonna be able to ride her bike anymore after this one. The cop was nice and said he's gonna give us SPEED ON BRIDGE violations instead of reckless driving...So what does that mean? I donno, but I gotta be more careful from now on.

Julie's Grandmother's party
Last night there was a big party at Townsquare Garden where close family and friend's of the Ha Truongs celebrated the 75th birthday their Grandmother. It was a great party with lots of good food, live bands, and performances.
Best moment that night for me was seeing my old friend Ben Lau and his daughter there. Ben Lau was my after school activity bus driver when I played basketball in 7th grade. We were good friends and I went over to his house to play with his daughter. Well after 7 years of not seeing him he is still doing what he loves, painting. He's actually a very talented artist and genius. It was a great reunion with him and his daughter. Thank you to Julie for bringing us back together.
The worse moment for me that night was when I was forced to dance. Not only did they (my friends who like to embarass me) force me to dance where my parents could see me, but my dad came up and started dancing with us. That was so damn embarassing. I don't even dance at the Quest anymore, what makes them think that I would want to dance to Twist and Shout in front of my parents? -_- I shall seek revenge on the savages that were entertained by my embarassment!

300 mile ride = major ass cramp
Wow I just got back from riding today. Left the house at 7:30AM and met up with Nick and Bud for a longass ride. I put 300 miles on it today going down to Winona.
