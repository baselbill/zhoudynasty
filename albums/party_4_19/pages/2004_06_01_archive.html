Monday, June 14, 2004

Big HDs make me drool
It's always fun to think back to 1994 and remember the 1.4GB capacity of storage space I had. I had two SCSI hard drives with 700MB each, man I was the king those days... I stored over a 300 pictures of Dragon Ball Z that I downloaded through AOL 2.5 using my 14.4kbps modem and 10 free hours of the initial membership. Yes, I did exceed those 10 hours, I think the first bill for the month was over $60. Unlimited internet access was still an unthinkable service back in those days.
I can't believe how fast technology advances. Today in 2004 I am configuring 200GB external hard drives that have firewire and USB connectivity. There are hundreds of these hard drives that need to be configured. Just the site of 30 of these hard drives in the storage room makes me drool.
Isn't it great how we have it today? We have 200GB hard drives and unlimited high-speed internet access. No we don't download Dragon Ball Z pics anymore, that would take too fast, we download entire episodes and movies now.
