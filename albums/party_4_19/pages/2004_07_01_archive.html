Tuesday, July 13, 2004

Dodgeball
We played our first scrimmages in the dodgeball league yesterday. It was a ton of fun... brought back the adrenaline felt in middle school. Except this time, the balls actually hurt, they're not like those foamy balls, and they fly a lot faster. We scrimmaged against 2 teams for a quite a few games and we came out on top for most of them. I think I realy like Dodgeball cuz you its great for anger management. Nothing beats the scared look in your opponent's eyes right before you throw that small dodgeball. Then the sound of the ball making contact against the flesh of your opponent, SMACK!.... ohhhh that just gets me excited. No wonder I always had fun throwing rocks at small animals when I was a kid.
We did have an accident though. During one game I while Andrea was running to pick up a ball as her opponent was doing the same, I ran behind her, out of the view of her opponent. When he opponent bent down to pick up the ball, I let my dogeball go as hard as I could with a running start. But at that split second, Andrea picked up a ball and lifted her head...SMACK! Well it wasn't really a direct hit on her head, more of a deflection. But enough impact to leave her seeing some stars. I felt pretty bad and it wasn't the first time I hit a girl in the face. Once I kicked a soccer ball as hard as I could and it smacked my cousin right in the face, dead center, she cried.
I'm really sore, I didn't know playing dodgeball would make you sore. Can't wait to do it again.

Saturday, July 10, 2004

In Love
I got up this morning and picked up the new battery for my bike. We put it and had a hell of a time trying to get it to start. Finally after several failed attempts and many tears, my dad and the neighbor pushed me on my bike downhill and finally got the thing to start. Man I can't describe how it felt when I was on my bike and felt it come alive again. I felt like I was flying and I was on the biggest high. I never realized how awesome my bike was until I had it stolen and was forced to ride a different model. The 600RR is just amazing compared to the R6. Later my dad and I went out for a long cruise. It was the first time we ever rode together, both without bike insurance and only one of us having a valid license. It was wonderful a ride, among the nice roads we hit, we also saw a lot of other bikers and found a nice little dinner out in the middle of no where. There are now two motorcycles (2003 Honda CBR600RR & 2001 Yamaha R6) in my garage and just that site makes me drool.
