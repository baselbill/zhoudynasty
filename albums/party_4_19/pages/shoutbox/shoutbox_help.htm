BB codes
The standard BB codes [U], [B], and [I] tags are for underlining, bolding, and italicizing your message, respectively.
Auto URL parsing is on and you can also specify the link message by using [URL=http://domain.com]message[/URL]
If it is enabled, you can also use the [IMG] tags to post an image. The usage is [IMG]http://domain.com/image.name[/IMG]
