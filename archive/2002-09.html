Archive: September 2002

School So Close So

School so close! So....I move in on Sunday, school starts on Tuesday. There's so much to do! Agh! I'm almost done packing, I gotta pack my clothes tomorrow and then get to UV before they close at 2 in order to get my keys. Then I gotta mow the lawn sometime on Monday, just too much. Anyways, after I move in, you guys are gonna have to come check out my pad. I think you'll find it quite like a study lounge..hehe. Confession On the other hand, I just got done playing a lot of CS. Well, a lot compared to none at all this whole summer. I'm leaving my computer tomorrow so I thought why not do whatever I want with my baby just one last night. So I did. Does that make me a bad man? Well I hope not. I'm still gonna go through with my plan of not playing any pc games the whole fall semester and spring. I didn't play throughout the summer and I already feel a change within me. I feel like I'm a better person because of it. I've felt more social and I have more fun doing other things, it's pretty good to stay away from pc games. Those games are like drugs, especially CS, it's more addicting than heroin, not that I've ever tried heroin.

Weekend Update Yeah I Forgot To Talk

Weekend Update Yeah I forgot to talk about my weekend... Saturday night I got drunk with Arash, Justin, Steve, and some other friends here at UV. Oh yeah my cousin Jon also came to visit me, he hung out with us for a while. He's going to Carlton now. Anyways, we all got drunk and went to a house party. I don't think there was much going on there, just a bunch of people drinking and hanging out on the porch. Then after that we went to Phi Psi and not much was going on there either. Just a bunch of people drunk/drinking and hanging out on the lawn. We went on their porch again, but left after like 30 mins. I refused to go home that night because Jaime didn't want to come over and see me and it was our 22nd month anniversary! So I sat on the curb with Steve and Jimmy, Andrea and some of friends came along. I think I insulted some people, sorry to you all that I made fun of while I was drunk. I didn't realize it until Steve told me the next day. I think I made some drunk phone calls to people too, I'm not sure. Oh yeah, we also went to PC Palace and bugged the nerdy kids there...haha. We had vodka on our breaths and we made fun of them playing Counter-Strike. Later on that night, like 1:30AM, Ellen and her boyfriend Harry stopped by to visit our apartment. Unfortunately, I don't remember much of what happened or what we said. All I know is that I was really tired and probably passed out after they left. Yep, that was my weekend update. I think we're gonna party a little again this Saturday after I get off work from Peking Way.

Weekend Yeah it was a pretty good weekend. Last night was the ASU Kickoff weekend and a lot of people went to the St. Paul Student Center. Dim Sum And New Friend This Morning I

Dim Sum and a new friend This morning I woke up at 10:30 and went to dim sum with Jaime at 11:30 along with Catherine and some of her friends. We met a guy named David, he just transferred here from Madison and he's studying Graphic Designs. It's weird cuz David lives in UV and when Jaime and I came down in the elevator he was in there with us. David is a cool guy, hope he'll get to hang out and party with us this semester. Oh did I mention that he's also from the same province that I was from? He moved to the US when he was 9. Confession I played CS today. After dim sum Arash called me and told me to go to PCP.
