Archive: November 2002

Birthday Things Had Great Time On My

Birthday Things Had a great time on my birthday. For dinner, Jaime, Jimmy, Andrea, Steve, Julie, David and Levong took me to Stewart Anderson's Cattle Company for steak. The steak was awesome and Levong and I had a bottle of wine! Then we went to Tropix with a group of 8 people I think. It was me, Julie, Steve, Lisa, Danny, David, Danny's friend John and Jean. Yeah it was a lot of fun, I even got drunk. People at Trpoix were pretty skanky though. CAAM Dance Went to see CAAM's annual performance this weekend on Saturday. It was really cool to see all the old CAAM people and to see Pat dancing in the production. Some parts of it was kind of boring, I fell asleep. After we got back from that show, we stopped by Dinnaken where Andrea and Jimmy and threw me a surprise party! But it was too bad that they were too drunk themselves...hehe they didn't even get me drunk. Anyways, my laptop is supposed to arrive today. I hope it's here by the time I get back from class! Great To See People It Was Great To

Great to see people It was great to see people over at our apartment tonight. Julie, Amy, Chuck, and Jenny stopped by W312 and we all ate dinner together from Dragon Court! It was a lot of fun. Amongst the visitors were Xai, Ryan, and Chee. I think some pictures were taken too...I will share them sometime on the site. PC Games New rule: NO CS IN THE APARTMENT!!! Steve and I have officially banned Counter-Strike from being played in our room. One night we stayed up till 4:30AM playing that evil game. First thing I did in the morning after missing class was to delete that son of a bitch! Ugh I've had enough of that damn thing. But today I just downloaded Madden 2002 and Monopoly Tycoon from the Kazaa! Damn those games are fun! They show up really nice on my laptop too. I just hope I don't get hooked on these games, I shouldn't because they're not first person shooters. currently thinking: damn sushi is good. my eyes are tired but i sure love my lappy. khoung thinks i'm a high-roller? lol cant wait to try age of mythology and new surfing game.

Uu Im Tired

U_U I'm tired. Sitting in the EE2361 lab right now by myself checking email. Just though I'd write a little something. Steve and I went to our 9 o'clock weight lifting class and we lifted pretty hard. Now we're both tired and I'm sitting here contemplating whether or not I should go to my discussion at 12:20. If you got a min, try this! Don't cheat by reading all the steps, just follow each step or you'll ruin the surprise. 1. How many times would you like to eat out for dinner in a week? Pick something from 1 to 7. 2. Now take that number of meals and double it. 3. Ok now add 5 more to that number of meals. 4. Got it? Ok now multiply this new number by 50...I'll wait for you to grab the calculator. 5. Ok now if you already had your birthday this year, add 1752. If you haven't, add 1751. 6. Now last step, subtract this number by the year in which you were born. 7. Ok now you should get a 3 digit number. The first digit is how many times would you like to eat out for dinner in a week. The last two digits is your age!
