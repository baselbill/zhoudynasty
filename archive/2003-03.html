Yesterdays Misfortunes I Had Pretty

Yesterday's Misfortunes I had a pretty rough day yesterday. All because of the lack of sleep and little bad luck. Started with me trying to make ramen in the morning. I put a cup of ramen in our microwave without water. It was until 2 minutes later that I smelled something funny. I turned around and there was thick smoke just pouring out of my microwave. I opened up the microwave to get of that ramen but that only released the smoke into the entire living room. I ended up opening my windows and turning on all the fans. I even had to stand under the fire alarm to wave at the smoke so it doesn't set it off. That was rough, and I ended up smelling like burnt ramen the whole the day. Then I went to class and I thought maybe I'll take a little nap because I felt so damn tired and couldn't concentrate. So I slept for like 5 mins and I woke up to my professor saying "You know it's funny how it's the same people who sleep ever day in lecture. That was so wrong because it was the first time I ever fell asleep in there. I think he's asian-blind and mistaken me for Steve. Steve slept through the whole thing and didn't even hear the prof make fun of me. Old School Oh my god I haven't seen a movie this funny for a while. Old School with Will Ferrell is hillarious.
