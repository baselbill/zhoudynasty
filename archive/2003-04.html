But yes I am going to the PSA fasion show, I think. Damn I have to work tomorrow. And then I have a midterm on Monday and one on Wednesday, got a lot of stuff to learn before then. Psa Fasion Show Last Minute Party So

PSA Fasion Show & Last Minute Party So the fashion was great on Friday. But the after party was even better! After we saw the PSA show, I asked (coerced) Andrea to have a party at her place. It was the one of the best last minute parties I've ever had. I think just about everyone that was cool attended. Andrea and I made a mad dash to the local liquor store. With that I was able to get everyone drunk...haha. But I feel a little bad about Matt because I wasn't watching him very closely and he ended up not being able to drive home, I heard he puked too. Alright this Thursday we're going clubbing...and I think there's plans for Friday but you will have to call me for the details on that...hehe.

Suckage So I Took Second Ee3115

Suckage So I took the second EE3115 midterm on electronic circuits yesterday. It sucked ass...I bombed that thing. I have good intelligence that I bombed it. Reason being it was hard and I partied too much to be able to catch up in one night. So speaking of bombing, did you hear they bombed Saddam last night and they think he's dead? Must be a mess over there. Last night I dreampt about weird stuff, a lot of unflushable shits and babies turning into rats that hang up side down. Yeah I know, doesn't make sense but that's some of the things that I dreampt about. So I got another test in transmission lines tomorrow, I hope I do bomb that one. I also have pretty good intelligence that I will do poorly on this one as well. Oh well after that it's seafood night at Mystic!

Sweaty Quest So Last Night Was

In the end my shirt was soaked and people were afraid to touch me cuz I was a sponge.

75 Degrees Studying When I Sat Down

75 degrees != studying When I sat down today and started doing homework, dchengo (David) messaged me and said "dude it's 75 degrees out." Never say that to me when I'm trying to study. Yes, we went to Lake Calhoun to rollerblade after he said that to me. Jimmy came with us too. It was fun trying out my new blades, they're a lot faster than my previous ones. A new discovery today, David sucks at rollerblading cuz he's only done it once! Poor guy was going slower than senior citizens taking a walk. But David got better, and I'm sure we'll do it again.

Asians Gone Wild

Asians Gone Wild... Party last night that David and I threw was a success. By success I mean that the cops came at around 2 and had to break it up. Luckly we didn't get a $700 fine and no one got a citation, everyone just had to leave. Couple of bad things: I got a little too drunk and my behavior was out of control. I'm sure I have a lot of girls to apologize to today, damn I feel like an idiot. A few pukers, not sure who other than David's cousin Ron but I know there are more pukers. Steve and I came back up to our apartment to find that someone took a shit or something in our toilet and clogged it. Piece of shit fuckin housemates who let other people use our toilet are not cool and I am pissed. Linh and her boyfriend broke up. But that is most likely a good thing that happened. I don't know what happened, but all $160 worth of liquor was gone, completely gone. I left my bartending post at midnight and when I got back just another hour and a half later, everything was gone. Every bottle was empty, that's just crazy. No wonder everyone was dancing last night. I'm glad everyone got to come and have a good time. I met some new people too. I'm sure I'll see them again, at another one of our parties.

Summer Is Coming So Now Im Not Sure

Summer is coming So now I'm not sure if I'm going to China this summer. This SARS thing is gonna make it hard for me to get back there because of the airlines being so tight. I'm not so afraid of the disease as I am of the hassle of traveling there. Also Nate is coming back to MN this summer for sure and it would be great to spend some time with him cuz he was in Cali last summer. Then I got my relatives from Penn coming to visit during 4th of July weekend, that'll be the bomb cuz we'll take the boat out all day probably. And also if I stay here I can do my favorite thing which is to get up in the morning and take summer classes!

Weekend Summary Friday I Went To White

Weekend Summary >Friday I went to a white people's wedding with Lisa. One of Lisa's good friends was getting married and she's in the ceremony so she needed a date for the occasion. It was pretty cool. The ceremony was kind of weird cuz it was all church like and I didn't know what the heck they were singing, I just felt out of place. Oh well at least no one pointed and laughed at me. The reception was pretty cool, we had some good food and champagne and wine. Then the white peoples started dancing to polka and swing, I thought that was interesting. I tried swing dancing a little bit but didn't have enough alcohol in my system to get down and polka. After we got back from the wedding we went directly to Ryan's house party all dressed up. It was pretty fun, the asians were dancing and drinking, the whites were watching the Wilds hockey game and drinking..hehe. >Saturday This is the day I finally got insurance activated again on my BMW. Thank god cuz it was a nice day out. I went to CASA's picnic, it was a great time. The board members arranged for an ambush with waterballoons and water guns on all the other members. It turned out to be one big ass water fight. A lot of people just got totally soaked, I think Josh got it all on his camcorder. We also played football and softball. Check out the pics and you'll see brave Dewitt retrieve the ball I hit onto the roof. But then when he was gonna come down he realized that he didn't have enough energy so the rest of CASA had to help him down. After I got off work Saturday I went to Ben's party at his apartment. I brought Wu and his friend Preston. This was the first time that I ever hung out with Wu but he's a pretty cool guy, he can drink a lot. The party was great except it was too nice outside so a lot of people ended up sitting on the grass and smoking. I got home at some time, I forgot cuz I was drunk.
