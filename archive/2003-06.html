Restaurant Owners Son So My Parents

The restaurant owner's son So my parents went to Rainbow Resort up by Itasca for the weekend and they just got back. They had a great time catching fish and just relaxing while I had a great time taking over their restaurant. Too bad they're back, I was just starting to enjoy working in a Chinese restaurant ten hours a day. Such a nice day to work at the restaurant, 76 degree weather with people in shorts outside and so many little kids running around and screaming and making a mess inside my restaurant. not! So I will finally get my cable modem at home on June 3rd. Omg when I get that thing, the first I'm gonna do...well you already know, yes, play Counter-Strike until my eyes fall out! Oh my god I can't wait to wake up in the morning and go straight to my computer to play CS and not shower until I have to have contact with the outside world and real people....oh yeah....(drooling)

Billys Nightmare Omg Last Night I

Billy's Nightmare! Omg last night I didn't sleep very well at all. I have this incredible nightmare that made me feel like my heart was about to jump out of my chest! I don't know if you've heard of my UFO story yet but once I saw this ufo that looked like a big bright moon. Anyways, last night I dreampt that I was at a diner with some friends after a day of waterskiing. Then I saw out of the window the UFO that I had seen before in real life. So I pointed it out to someone and I said "Look! There's that ufo I had seen before, except this time it looks even bigger." Then this thing started to move, real fast across the horizon. It seemed like it knew that we had spotted the irregularities. I was like "Holy shit get inside!" Then this UFO started to circle around the dinner emitting some kind of light. Everyone was freaking out and I saying to myself "Am I dreaming or is this UFO gonna get us?" It seemed so real at the time, the fear, the table I was hiding under all seemed so real, it just freaked me out even more. Then this UFO stopped and landed infront of the diner in a saucer-like manner and started emitting some sort of light pulses at everyone. This was the worse part of the dream. Everyone was scared and thought they were going to die or get abducted. I knew this was my last chance to do anything before I die. So I ran out to the landed UFO and ripped a chunk out of it. Then I ate it. And it tasted good! It tasted like this Chinese bread I used to eat. Crispy outside and really soft of the inside and kind of sweet. Then I just helped myself to another server of the UFO bread and ate some more. Then my mom came out and started helping me to eat this tasty UFO. Before you know it, the UFO was defeated and everyone was saved! Then I woke up and I was really hungry.

What Nice Day Wow Its So Nice Out

What a nice day! Wow it's so nice out today! Beautiful weather! And you know how that makes me feel. My dad and I are taking out the boat today for the first time this season, I finally get to even out my tan. ^^ Today was also my first day of school for the summer. I am taking Statistical Methods and Beginning Piano. Stats is so damn boring it makes me want to puke. Piano was pretty cool but I almost fell asleep while playing the notes because I had to wake up at 6 this morning. Recap on CS Tourney So yeah, we didn't take first place. Or second, or third, or fourth. We did pass the first round though! In the second round we played a really tough opponent who ended up taking 2nd place and only losing by 2 rounds. Up until today, I thought we made it to the semi-finals, but I was wrong. That's ok though, it was a lot of fun to compete and put your skills and teamwork to the test. I do have to admit that there were some teams there with a lot of skill and plenty of experience. That's where we lack. Maybe I'll sacrifice more of my social life to get good at this game and be ranked somewhere along the top.
