I Wetted Myself And Then I Wetted

I wetted myself, and then I wetted myself again So I got my motorcycle today! It is awesome! It's a Honda and it's yellow. When they delivered it today it only had a gallon of gas in it. I was so afraid to drive it to the gas station cuz I heard it's really powerful and stuff. But then I said what the heck I'll just drive 5 mph to get gas for it. As soon as I hopped on, this thing made me feel at home. I felt like I had so much control over it. So I got gas and it was like only $6. Then instead of coming back home, I don't know what took over me but I drove all around Woodbury and even pushed it to 65 mph! Then I came back home and my hands were trembling. I don't know if it's from the powerful engine or if it's from a delayed adrenaline rush. I took the bike out again later in the after and went over 70 on some country roads, I can't even describe how awesome it feels. It's almost like flying, you're going fast but you're on the ground. You Are Under Arrest So Tonight We

You are under arrest So tonight we went to the quest. IT was cool cuz I haven't been there in a while and everyone was going. Some of us decided to meet up at Grandma's before hand to take advantage of their happy hour. So we got a little buzzed then went up to Karen's place in Grandmarc and got even more plastered. Then me, Andrea, Nate, Luise and David all rode in one car to go clubbing. We were smoking cigars and puffing out smoke. Then the car behind us put their brights on and I was like "wtf?!" and I gave them the finger. Then the police lights came on and they pulled us over. Two cops came over to me and yanked me out of David's car and handcuffed me then searched me. Lol I was like what the hell what did I do? I thought it was really funny cuz all I did was give them the finger and they were swearing at my and pushing me around. Then they threw me in the back of their squad car with these two black kids. One of them had a warrant and the other was the accomplace. I talked to them in the back of the car, I said I didn't do anything and they said they didn't do anything either then the cops told us to shut up. Anyways the cops took us to some jail in Minneapolis and dropped off the two kids. Then I talked to the cops and we got into what high school they graduated from and stuff. It was really funny, I actually got to know them pretty well. Then they said they were going to 'unarrest' me. So they took off my handcuffs (which hurt a lot) and then they drove me to the Quest and dropped me off. I even shook their hands and thanked them for arresting me cuz it was my first time and good experience. Overall, it was a wonderfuly experience while drunk. I wish I could remember more so I can post it. My parents think it was funny I got arrested cuz my grandpa once got arrested too and he would've been proud that I talked my way out of it. So Addy and Sarah drove me back. Damn I feel bad that they had to drive me so far. Thanks girls! PS. I am still pissed drunk and I gotta wake up for my morning class tomorrow.

929 Miles So Far Since July 1St Ive

929 Miles! So far since July 1st I've put 929 miles on my bike! Today I met someone on a motorcycle website who lives in Maple Grove. Her name is Sara and she rides a blue Yamaha YZF. I think I will be riding with their group in the future. Then later this evening I went to the CASA board dinner at AZIA. What the hell is AZIA you ask?! It's a contemporary Chinese restaurant with a style of Asian American fusion cooking. I got this Grilled Sea Bass dish. The menu said it comes with a forest of broccoli. When they said forest, they weren't kidding! The whole damn plate was covered with broccoli and it was put together to resemble a forest. Wow, I've always hated broccoli and now I hate it even more. The sea bass was pretty good but it was kind of burnt, I guess that's the azian style.

Hooters Last Night I Took My Parents

Hooters Last night I took my parents to Hooters for their happy hour. They were amazed that I can eat so many wings. Oh we tried the raw oysters, oyster roast, and steam clams too. Here's my review. Raw Oysters: 10/10 They are absolutely awesome! You think the raw oysters at the Mystic Lake seafood buffet is good? Well these oysters put them to shame! Put on your bib Khuong cuz when you get these oysters, they're fresh and juicy. Not a hint of deadness. Their oysters actually taste like it comes from the ocean. They're served with the cocktail sauce, melted butter, horse raddish, lemon wedges and crackers. I don't know why crackers, maybe I don't know the art of eating raw oysters. They were $9.95 for 12. A little pricy, but well worth every penny if ya like em oysters fresh and clean! Oyster Roast: 7/10 They come in a bucket. And they're fresh too! They're a lot of fun to eat cuz the Hooters girl gives you a towl and this tool for shucking them. You have to open the oysters yourself like a fisherman. The only drawback with these is that they come all shrivelled up cuz they're cooked. But it's a lot of fun to shuck them. $11.95 for 18. Steam Clams: 6/10 Well I'm a little bias but if you ask me, they would taste better raw! These clams weren't special, they weren't cooked in a sauce or anything, just steamed. They did taste a little spicy. $9.95 for 13. Hope that was helpful to you seafood lovers out there. Hey if you're heading out next time for Hooters wings and oysters, gimme a call!
