Back In

Back in...school So here I am, at work. It's about 8:30AM, I've answered two calls and I am anxiously waiting for the stock market to open. I had a great Thanksgiving break in Las Vegas and I hope everyone else had a good one too. Hopefully no one spent too much time studying, cuz that would suck. Vegas We arrived in Vegas on late Wednesday night, after eating some late night food and scoping out the Aladdin hotel, Kenneth and I decided to go to be at around 4AM while my mom decided to do a little slot action. I woke up the next morning at 8AM and my mom was no where in sight. I called her because I was worried something had happened. Turns out that she hit a jackpot on the quarter slots for $1250...so naturally she didn't feel like sleeping. As my mom came back to the room to sleep, Kenneth and I headed out for lunch at Todai. It was one of the best lunches I've had in a long time. We ate so much sushi and seafood and topped it off two big bottles of Kirin's. Kenneth and I gambled too, played some blackjack and roulette. We both came away pretty even. If it wasn't for my luck in roulette I'd be screwed. The rest of the trip included more great food, shopping, and gambling. Even a buffet at 4AM, after Kenneth and I failed to get into the hottest club in Vegas (Rain) because they don't open on Thanksgiving. We also saw Eason Chan while we were shopping in the mall of Aladdin. Yeah it was all good, I still have to post the pictures. Time to get back to work.
