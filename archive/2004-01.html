Happy Chinese New Year Gong Xi Fa Cai

Happy Chinese New Year! Gong xi fa cai! I am celebrating Chinese New Year this weekend with my family. My dad came back from Xiamen 3 days ago and my grandma came back with him. Last night we went to Edina to celebrate the new years with a bunch of distant relatives, mostly my dad's cousins and their families. It is always a big event because there are so many people, but I don't know everyone. One weird thing I noted was that all of my dad's cousins married a white person, except for one of them. I think it is because they grew up in America and were not exposed to enough Chinese culture because they were picked on a lot at school. There was also a really cute girl from China, she is adopted and she's only 4. Her mother went to China and adopted her, then moved to her hometown in the country side for a year and taught English. Now the little girl goes to a Chinese school in Roseville 3 days out of the week where they only speak Chinese. Her mother is also planning to take her back every 2 years. I think that's great, I only wish more adoptees from China had this kind of opportunity to be in touch with their roots. I wish I was in China right now, it seems like they are just having a great time, everyone is in the spirit. Being in America is hard but I'm lucky to have family here.

Chinese I Spent My Entire Weekend With

Chinese I spent my entire weekend with Mom, Dad and Grandma. I was completely emersed in Chinese. I spoke and heard Chinese 99% of the time this weekend, even the tv and movie we saw over the weekend was all in Chinese. I took my grandma to Yummy's for dim sum today and had a good talk about some of the family history. She told me that her father was the first person in China to grow strawberries. He came to America before WW2 to study agriculture. So he then brougt back the knowledge of some kind of rubber substance I think and also strawberries! She also told me how her father was persecuted and beaten during the Communist Revolution. All that talk about China in Chinese made me forget where I was. I thought I was in China, especially since I was eating dim sum and it was pretty good. Tomorrow I will go to work and return to speaking a lot of English, blah. The Magic of Google With the help of Google I have gotten to meet some interesting people over the internet. I have met a very special friend in Malaysia and a wonderful family in Xiamen, and not to mention the scores of students doing research on the Zhou Dynasty. Google continues to advance and I believe it will be second nature to everyone in a few years, just like the remote control.
