Hows Work Many People Often Ask Me How

How's work? Many people often ask me how my job has been..."How's work?"...."What's it like to be working full time?" My answers are usually a quick "Pretty good, not bad." That's just cuz I don't want to go into detail about it. But I will today for those of you who want to know more. Here's my typical day... I set my alarm for 7 and usually don't leave the house until 8:15 because I need to eat a little something for breakfast. This week has been the turkey sandwiches with the Chinese satay sauce and the cock brand hot sauce..yum. Well I usually get to work and have my laptop on at 9. I do a wide variety of things such as checking my email, read news sites, find interesting websites, check my stocks, blog, and also walk around and look for hotties in the building. Haven't found too many hotties yet, but there are a lot of people here so I will eventually find a couple. I usually go out to lunch with my calorie-counting friend Ryan (who lost more than 80 pounds in 3 months) and we spend like 90 mins just eating and talking. Lunch is good and feels much better because we actually have some money to spend. For the rest of the day I might use my brain a little for work related activies, and I mean little. When 4 o'clock rolls around I start to pack up because its time to go home... And then the crappy part about work, the traffic. The traffic is ridiculous after work and is unpredictable. That's about the only crappy thing. Everyone in my department is pretty cool and very easy going. They do swear and make fun of people quite a lot more than I had imagined in the "professional environment" but I've gotten used to it and have joined them. So I guess I really enjoy working, but not commuting. If I can give a piece of advice to an intern it would be to go out and enjoy yourself. Don't ever work too hard because they're not expecting to. Spend your energy getting to know people, having fun and getting involved in company things that are free to you. And oh yeah, spend some time updating your blogs too. Just remember not to work too hard.

Summer Is Here It Was About This Time

Summer is here It was about this time of the year in '03 when Matt asked me what I'm gonna do this summer and my reply was "I donno, pick my nose?" Well this summer is a little different. I actually have a lot planned. I'm leaving for China in 7 days, I can't believe how fast time flies. I will be there for 3 weeks, then come back to stary my internship at Cargill. This summer I will be working at the Cargill the whole time but I still be living on campus so feel free to stop by. It is just amazing how fast time flies. About 4 months ago I started my internship at Rosemount and today was my last day already. I cleaned up my cube and said goodbye to everyone, and I left with the feeling of helplessness when it comes to controlling time. But there will always be better and more interesting things ahead, no regrets. Everyone wants me to keep updating my blog and pictures while I'm in China. I don't know how likely that will be but I will try, keep in mind I'm on vacation. I usually don't like to look at watches or go near computers during my vacation. But I guess this time I will have to check on my stocks and blog a little so its inevitable.
