Archive: July 2004

Bacon Owns Me Wow I Love Bacon

Bacon owns me Wow I love bacon. They have bacon at work for breakfast in the morning. Every morning I go down to the cafeteria to look at the thick cut yummy bacon but I don't buy it because it costs $0.45 a piece!!!!! So expensive! But this morning I was feeling good and its Friday so when I went down there this time, I actually bought some. I got 3 slices of bacon and the lunch lady thought that it was only 2 because 2 slices stuck together. So she only charged $0.90 instead $1.35, SCORE!! Yeah the bacon was good, I brought it back to my cube and ate it secretly or my co-workers would be drooling all over the place.

In Love I Got Up This Morning And

In Love I got up this morning and picked up the new battery for my bike. We put it and had a hell of a time trying to get it to start. Finally after several failed attempts and many tears, my dad and the neighbor pushed me on my bike downhill and finally got the thing to start. Man I can't describe how it felt when I was on my bike and felt it come alive again. I felt like I was flying and I was on the biggest high. I never realized how awesome my bike was until I had it stolen and was forced to ride a different model. The 600RR is just amazing compared to the R6. Later my dad and I went out for a long cruise. It was the first time we ever rode together, both without bike insurance and only one of us having a valid license. It was wonderful a ride, among the nice roads we hit, we also saw a lot of other bikers and found a nice little dinner out in the middle of no where. There are now two motorcycles (2003 Honda CBR600RR & 2001 Yamaha R6) in my garage and just that site makes me drool.

Dodgeball We Played Our First

Dodgeball We played our first scrimmages in the dodgeball league yesterday. It was a ton of fun... brought back the adrenaline felt in middle school. Except this time, the balls actually hurt, they're not like those foamy balls, and they fly a lot faster. We scrimmaged against 2 teams for a quite a few games and we came out on top for most of them. I think I realy like Dodgeball cuz you its great for anger management. Nothing beats the scared look in your opponent's eyes right before you throw that small dodgeball. Then the sound of the ball making contact against the flesh of your opponent, SMACK!.... ohhhh that just gets me excited. No wonder I always had fun throwing rocks at small animals when I was a kid. We did have an accident though. During one game I while Andrea was running to pick up a ball as her opponent was doing the same, I ran behind her, out of the view of her opponent. When he opponent bent down to pick up the ball, I let my dogeball go as hard as I could with a running start. But at that split second, Andrea picked up a ball and lifted her head...SMACK! Well it wasn't really a direct hit on her head, more of a deflection. But enough impact to leave her seeing some stars. I felt pretty bad and it wasn't the first time I hit a girl in the face. Once I kicked a soccer ball as hard as I could and it smacked my cousin right in the face, dead center, she cried. I'm really sore, I didn't know playing dodgeball would make you sore. Can't wait to do it again.

Lunch Break Ahh Much Needed Lunch

Lunch break Ahh a much needed lunch break today. With my car being at the shop and undriveable, I rode my motorcycle to work today. It is nice out today, the ride to work was great, I felt like I was in the matrix freeway scene with all that traffic, except I was going the same direction as the traffic, which is good. Lunch came and I took the loooonng way to Luck Inn, the Chinese buffet. The lunch break ride was much needed, I needed to free my mind and ride on something fast with great handling and acceleration. I had the buffet by myself and finished in no less than 15 minutes. Then the fortune cookie came and said "It's about time you asked that special someone on a date." Wow! But who the hell is that special someone? The damn fortune cookie forgot to tell me who the special someone is that I was suppoed to ask to go on a date with. I can't think of a special someone that I've been wanting to ask out, -_- which means I won't be going on any dates anytime soon.
