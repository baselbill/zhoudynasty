Dont Laugh At Your Superiors I Think I

Don't laugh at your superior(s) I think I just learned a lesson today. Don't laugh or make fun of your supervisor for the mundane tasks that he/she has to do. Your laughter and mockery only helps them to remember that they have an intern to push all the work onto. I have been copying and pasting stuff into a spreadsheet at 100 rows at a time, I am on the 2146th row and I have much more to go. My eyes are tired and my brain is dead. At least I found a way to do it slightly faster than my supervisor. -_-

Saggy Pants Downgraded Virginia Bans

Saggy pants downgraded Virginia bans low-rider pants Do you remember when it was fashionable to sag your pants so much that your boxers are showing? I don't remember if I did when I was in high school but I do remember some "gansta" Edina kids doing that. Man I thought it was so cool, I wanted to be just like them but I knew that it would grow on me and in a few years they'd pass a bill that would fine you for wearing saggy jeans. I also didnt sag because I wore whitey tighties back then and it wasn't as hot showing them because any brown/yellow stains would be easily noticed. ::snaps fingers:: Well if you sag your pants, better not do it in Virginia, you'll get fined $50 now. If I was a sagger in Virginia I would wear TWO pairs of pants. The outer layer to express my fashion statement by sagging and the inner layer to protect me from this new indecent exposure bill.

Bad Dream I Didnt Sleep Well At All

Bad dream I didn't sleep well at all last night. I had a pretty bad dream and it was so real. I dreampt that I parked my motorcycle outside on the street everyday. One day I looked out of the window of my apartment and some kids were riding my motorcycle around! They kept tipping it over and scratching it up! I was royally pissed off and ran after them. But for some reason I didn't beat them up. In real life I don't know if I could've controlled myself. Then when I rode my motorcycle again it felt weak and crappy. Then I kept saying to myself that it is just a dream, that I'd wake up and my bike will be just fine. Shortly I woke up with a huge sigh of relief, except for the fact that I had to get up and get to work. Blah

Almost Vegetarianated I Went To

Almost vegetarianated I went to Woodbury to check on the house Friday... I openned the door and there was a piercing stench that penetrated to the back of my throat. It smelled like something crawled into my house, died, came back alive and died again dripping wet in carcass fluids. I tried to breath with only my mouth but that didn't help. I looked around, barely breathing and found out that the refrigerator wasn't working. I opened it up and everything that was cold or frozen before was now at room temperature. It must've been like that for a good 3 or 4 days because all the blood had dripped down and hardened. Juices from the chicken, shrimp, beef, pork and other things all flowed together to form a mixture of foul smelling soup. There were no gloves in sight so I used my bare hands to remove all this stinky meat and into garbage bags. I filled up 4 garbage bags full of this rotten meat and tried to clean as much as I could. The worst part was that the rotten meat juices combined with the vanilla ice cream and flowed into the ice dispense. After cleaning all this crap up I had to load the bags into my car and drive it to our old restaurant. Since we cancelled our trash service, I had to dump it at the old Peking Way. The whole ordeal is over now, I think, who knows what else is in store for me. All the blood and rotten meat juices mixed together combined with that foul stench almost made me become a vegetarian. But since I love meat so much and would never dishonor it, I still eating it right now, as I am typing up this entry. This has nothing to do with the entry: WILL LO, WHAT IS YOUR PHONE NUMBER IN HONG KONG?

Cherish Everything You Have One Of My

Cherish everything you have One of my friends in Singapore is going to see his friend Blenheim in the hospital today, I asked him what's wrong, he pointed me to a blog where Blenheim's friend Tocky explained what happened, sort of. The blog is called "Impossible is Nothing" and here's a link to it. Tocky says this... "this is ths most terrible days of my life..guys and girls reading this...comparing to a relastionship break up with your gf and bf cannot not even compare to the feeling of losing a love one... really, it's terrible, TREASURE YOUR LOVED ONES...please do not wait till somehting happen then we start to make our first move... love is not periodic, there is no start or edn time to love, love is everythere, all the time....Oh God be with my brother...." I really have to agree with him. Most of the time I find myself wrapped up in my daily activities like work, school, and yes, them damn video games. I get so wrapped up that I forget about the most important thing in life, the people I love. I fail to communicate to them just how much I love and appreciate them. Tocky's good friend is now in a deep coma after having a clog in a major artery leading to his brain. He has a 10%-20% chance of surviving the 2nd surgery because the 1st one had failed. Imagine being able to hear your friends and family talking to you and praying for you but you can't even express how much you love them. Today I will call those people that I care about, those whom I've been too "busy" to call. I suggest you all do that, cherish what you have today, not tomorrow when you're not "busy".
