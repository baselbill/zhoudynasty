July 10, 2006 — posted by Billy

Raw oysters and IPA

Boulder is awesome, both during the day and night. I'm glad they've sent me to entertain the Chinese guests. We're staying at the Boulderado, which is right in downtown Boulder and a couple blocks away from the happenin' Pearl Street. Today we went through the art fair and checked out the farmer's market. With a high of 101 degrees, even those umbrellas from China weren't enough to keep our guests cool, they ended early to take a nap.

I felt a little bored sitting in my room watching TV and "Over the Hedge" (good movie) so I went out for a little drinky myself. I found a nice place called Boulder Cafe and odered a pint of locally brewed indian pale ale and a doze of fresh raw oysters. The IPA was perfect for the warm evening, crisp and hoppy. My Chesapeake and Kumamoto oysters were phenomenal, they were so fresh that I could taste the difference in each of them.

Sitting outside in the warm evening air while drinking locally brewed IPA and slurping down some fresh oysters and watching a street show makes really makes this a nice vacation. Too bad I don't have any one close to share my oysters with, more for me I guess.

July 16, 2006 — posted by Billy

Bill or Billy

Bill or Billy? What's better for my professional career? Over the weekend we had the 50th anniversary celebration at our company in which a video was shown to all employees and their family. The video was a compliation of our employees from around the world talking about what it means to work here. I was in the video, but since I was in Boulder I wasn't able to attend the celebration. That's good because I felt awkward during the video shoot and wasn't looking forward to seeing myself on the big screen. Anyway, I got back to the office today and people who saw the video thought it was great and some of them started calling me Billy... Because for some reason, my name in the video showed up as Billy rather than Bill.
