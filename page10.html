Im Back Yes Im Back From Hawaii It

I'm back Yes I'm back from Hawaii, it was a lot of fun and I am a lot of burnt by the sun. We stayed on Oahu, Big Island and Maui. Internet Again I Went Back Up To Nwa

Internet again I went back up to the NWA VIP lounge to drink some free beer and my parents snuck in with me. They have this Sapporo beer machine that is incredibly awesome. Then the machine tilts the glass at a 30 degree angle and starts pouring the beer. When it is almost full, it tilts the beer back to its original position and adds a bit of head to your glass.
