Monday, November 08, 2004

Weekend Lan We Had Our Lan Party In

Weekend LAN We had our LAN party in my basement on Saturday night. It was a wild. I think there were about 12 computers and 16 people throughout the night. We started off by playing a massive 8 player game of Age of Empires 2 and the power went out half way through the game. The power went out a second time for AOE2 when we tried to finish it. A lot of ass-kicking was done in UT and we also played some new games like Counter-Strike Source and the new Doom3. The gaming didn't end till 9AM and started at around 7PM. I am still recovering from the lack of sleep and images of people getting fragged still remains in my head.
