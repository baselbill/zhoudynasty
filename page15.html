Small Town Wedding Freshwater Fun This

Small town wedding freshwater fun This weekend Danny and I went up north to Hibbing for our friend Kate's wedding. Hibbing is a small town of 15,000 people located 3 and half hours north of the cities. The wedding was great, traditional and simple. It wasn't the most beautiful wedding I've seen but it sure was a lot of fun partying it up at the reception. The reception was at a restaurant called Elks Lounge. It occupied the entire 3rd floor of a building and it was big. There was a lot of good American food and an open bar. We started partying it up at 4PM and didn't end till 11PM.
