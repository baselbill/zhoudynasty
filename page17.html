Tired Why Do I Stay Up So Late

Tired Why do I stay up so late all the time? Damn I'm tired today. I found a new thing to do when there's some free time at work. Go to www.manadarintools.com and go to their Character Flashcard page. There you can learn the meaning of Chinese characters, how they sound like and how to write them. Then you can download Chinese character sheets, print them out and start practicing your writing. I'm trying to learn about 10 words a day. So far so good but my hand writing totally sucks. I feel like a little kid trying to draw. Ok that's my tip for the day, time to start fixing things.

Big Hds Make Me Drool Its Always Fun

Big HDs make me drool It's always fun to think back to 1994 and remember the 1.4GB capacity of storage space I had. I had two SCSI hard drives with 700MB each, man I was the king those days... I stored over a 300 pictures of Dragon Ball Z that I downloaded through AOL 2.5 using my 14.4kbps modem and 10 free hours of the initial membership. Yes, I did exceed those 10 hours, I think the first bill for the month was over $60. Unlimited internet access was still an unthinkable service back in those days. I can't believe how fast technology advances.

Eating Lunch Do You Ever Eat Lunch Or

Eating lunch Do you ever eat lunch or dinner by yourself? For some reason I can't go out and eat by myself. I've always had this problem ever since I can remember. Anyways, today for lunch I was going to go get a sub to bring back. But I found a little Chinese restaurant called Lucky Inn. I knew it had a buffet and it was a family style restaurant and I knew that I'm tired of eating those buffet things, but for some reason I couldn't help myself and I went to eat there. The food wasn't bad and the price is ok, but the buffet is small compared to my family's buffet. The whole time I was eating I was wondering about who owns this place and whether or not I know him/her since everyone knows each other here in MN. So does your family own Luck Inn in Eden Prairie?

Attention Xanga Slaves Users It Has

Attention Xanga Slaves (Users)!! It has been brought to my attention that the poor souls over in Xanga land must pay in order to host their own images. I've always enjoyed posting up images on my blog for free, but that's because I have my own server. I think posting mages with your blog entry is really cool and really adds a lot more content.
