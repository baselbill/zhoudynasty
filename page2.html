Friday, June 16, 2006

Been a long week

Actually the past 3 weeks have been crazy at work. My calendar now looks like something I could never have imagined when I was an intern. This week is by far the worst as I just got done with 5 days of non-stop training. Being at training this long is terrible to my productivity, it's like taking a 5 day vacation and getting nothing done except it's worse because training is nothing close to vacation. I need to get re-energized because it's going to get worse for the rest of this month and next month. I'm too tired to even drive home right now, I think I'm going to sit here in silence. Friday's late afternoon silence is very comforting.

Sunday, June 11, 2006

Hit by a golf ball

I got hit by a golf ball today while playing 9 holes with Josh and Al. As we were getting ready to tee off, we heard a "fore!" and as I turned around I heard a ball flying through some branches and leaves and in a split second it landed right in my lower back. I was stunned a bit and just stood there as I thought to myself "what a shitty shot". If that ball had hit me on the head, I think I would have a huge bump on my forehead right now. We let those dangerous golfers play through and on the following hole, Josh almost hit them with his drive. Try harder next time Josh.

Thursday, June 08, 2006

My lost friends...

Steven Blakemore, Jeremy Goins, and Mathew Nelson, where are you guys? Steven, Jeremy and Mathew were my closest friends for my first 4 years in America. I really long for these friends that I left in AZ when I moved to MN, I wish we would've kept in touch. Back in 1994 when we moved to Minnesota, the internet wasn't so widely available, actually I never even knew about it. I kept in touch with my friends through writing actual paper letters. Over the course of a couple years, some of us moved and that's where the connection broke. I hope to reunite with them again someday and everyday the chances are greater due the world getting smaller.

Sims 2 money cheat

It's Monday, I'm still at work, and Amanda just called me to ask how to do the money cheat for Sims 2. Incase you are at home and about to call someone to ask for the Sims 2 money cheat, just do the following: hold down SHIFT and CTRL and hit C. Then enter in "motherlode" and hit ENTER.
