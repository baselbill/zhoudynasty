Intern Life Wow I Am Somebody At This

Intern life Wow I am a somebody at this company now. They just gave me my name plate to place by my cube. It says "Bill Zhou" and they made it just for me. Yay! Before I had a piece of paper taped on the wall with my name and then my supervisor's name on it. I felt like I was somebody's slave. I think I still am. Within my half-sized cube I have everything I need; a phone, stabler, tape, pens, cleanex, a laptop, and my nalgene bottle.
