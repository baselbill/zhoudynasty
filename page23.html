Steak Last Night After My Night Class

Steak Last night after my night class I went to the Steak Knife with my old friend Kenneth. This place is not bad. The prices for the steaks were decent (college budget) and the ambience was also quite nice. We had a few beers and watched a live band play. I didn't know the name of the band but they were cool too, even got one of those guys who rap freestyle. The night before last night I went to Ichiban's with Danny and John. John is going to Mexico to be immersed in Spanish for the next 5 months. We had tons of great sushi and sake. Afterwards we went to Sally's to have a few drinks and then came back to Dinnaken to play Texas Hold 'em where David lost like $20.

Mentoring I Met With My Mentor From Mg

Mentoring I met with my mentor from M G again last night. He is one great guy. We talked for an hour and a half just about what he and some of the lawyers do there. It is amazing how fast-paced and hard working IP lawyers are. Their organizational structure is not a functional one, it is more of a client focused organization. There are client mangers and below them they can allocate their attorneys in working on their cases. He also treated me to dinner, again.
