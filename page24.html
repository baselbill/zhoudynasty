Wicking Shirt So I Bought This Reebok

Wicking shirt So I bought this Reebok sports shirt at Marshalls like two months ago for $5. It is supposed to wick away your sweat and keep you cool. I got it because I plan on wearing it this summer when I start riding my motorcycle again. Well I wore that shirt inside my normal long sleeve today. Boy does that thing make you feel cool! I have a jacket on and when I move around, I can just feel all the coolness. I didn't expect it to have that kind of effect on my body. It also doesn't help since it was -24F outside this morning.

Cold It Is Cold In Minnesota

Cold It is cold in Minnesota. Low of -16F (-26C) and a high of -5F (-20C). This morning when I was going to work the wind chill was at -33F (-36C) !!!! My car growled at me. I turned my key to start the ignition and kept it turned because my car tried to die on me again. I don't know how much longer the 89 Audi is going to hold up in this weather.
