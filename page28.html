Last Samurai I Just Got Back From

The Last Samurai I just got back from watching The Last Samurai, an epic film about the ways of the samurai fading as Japan transitions into a modern civilization. This movie is great, I have not seen a film as inspiring, thoughtful, and true as this. They just don't make movies like these anymore, you all should definately see it, especially if you are a man. Also I've addressed this a few times to people who think that the movie is mainly about Tom Cruise being the last samurai. It is actually about the forgotten ways of the samurai.
