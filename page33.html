Big Yeah Before I Left My Apartment

BIG YEAH!!! Before I left my apartment tonight to go slave away at the computer lab I checked my email once more... I got a reply from Terra the recruiter for Emerson Process Management telling me that I have been selected to go for the onsite interview. And I remember during the interview she told me that if they select you, you would go onsite and meet with them and by the end of the day they would make you an offer. So YEAH! I got accepted for the co-op position! I am so excited because this was the company that I was interested in the most. I'm in, unless if I screw up. I think it'll be harder to screw this one up than the interview.
