I Fell Again Ok Heres What Happened

I fell, again Ok here's what happened.. I decided to go for a little ride before my friend came over to meet me. I was riding around the U of M campus and decided to take a turn that I normally don't take. I think my entry speed was a little fast for that turn because I didn't expect this turn to be more than 90 degrees. So I said 'ok I can handle this, I've been reading Twist 2 by Keith Code'. I rolled on the throttle and leaned, I had to lean more than usual. Then I felt my bike starting to slide. I put my my right hand on the asphalt as I slid, that was hot, so then I lifted my hand off.

Wrenching I Worked On My Bike Today

Wrenching I worked on my bike today with my friend Sheldon. I drove over the Woodbury and we installed Yoyodyne frame sliders on my bike! It was a lot of fun working with him and it was the first time meeting him. The sliders look real nice and fit perfectly. We had to cut a hole 2 inches in diameter on both sides of the fairing, that was scary! Well now that I have the sliders on, if my bike tips over again, it won't damage my fairings. Too bad I couldn't put them in yesterday. =(
