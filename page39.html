Weekend Getaway Well

Weekend getaway Well...not quite. Nate and I went camping Saturday. We got a spot at Lake Elmo Park reserve. It was an awesome night packed with roasting hot dogs and marshmellows, watching the stars and the northern lights, and of course, talking. Seems like Nate and I never run out of things to ponder about. For lunch Nate and I drove to Edina for Chico's Taco. Only to find out that it went out of business! =( Boo-who! So we went to Fudruckers instead and that wasa great food. We even saw Tony Rosen's dad there. Then later on tonight I went to see Charlie's Angels 2.
