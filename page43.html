Spring Break Party My White Friend

Spring Break Party My white friend Ryan had this party at his house on campus to kick off the start of the spring break. It was a pretty cool party, especially since it was another asian invasion. There were a lot of asians there, I guess the calls I made were effective in getting people to come. Anyways I think I had a little too much to drink that night, I hope no one was offended by anything I did. And I know I must've done something because it's impossible for me to get drunk and not do something or say something stupid. Hope you all had some fun, maybe we'll do it again next time without the white population, who don't even like to dance.

Quest Like Never Before So Tonight Was

Quest like never before So tonight was the first night ever that I went to the Quest while being a single guy. It's so different, you're always dancing with other girls and stuff. Feels a little bit intimidating dancing with girls that you don't know, I still have to work on that.
