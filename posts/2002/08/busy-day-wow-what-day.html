Busy Day Wow What Day

Busy day Wow what a day...I did so much stuff. Got up at like 11 and went to Jaime's house with 2 big tubs of soup to fight her fire. hehe After spending some time at the Chou's house, I met up with my mom and Danny at COSTCO. And guess what? Danny bought another shirt. Then after some shopping, the 3 of us headed to Mystic Lake for their seafood night. It was a weird night there, I didn't ravage the buffet. Usually I go to Mystic and I'm so hungry, hungry beyond the point of starvation. But today I get there and I'm like "I'm not hungry." It sucked because I ate slow and didn't pig out on the seafood. Instead, I would eat a plate, sit back and watch other people eat. In the end I felt neither stuffed nor hungry, it was dissapointing. Usually I go in there starving and come out ready to explode, but I guess it's a bad buffet day for me. The same thing happened to Danny. We played some black jack afterwards, I quickly saw Danny lose $20. Then my mom played the slots and I think she won some money at the end when we were trying to leave. You know, they always start winning at the end when it's time to leave. I already got my car, and she was still in there, the machine just didn't want to let her go. Last night's nightmare Last night I had this Lord of the Rings-like dream...it was scarry as hell. There was this huge world war with 7 other countries and mine was one of them. Anyways to make a long story short, I tried to cut a guy's head off to finish him quick but I just barely kill him. First try only severs half of his neck, second strike cuts off his chin, and then finally I kill the poor bastard. What a nightmare! Camping Yes I will be gone and up in the Boundry Waters starting from tomorrow till Monday. It's gonna be a hell of a fun time up there fishing and all. I promise to not forget to bring my digital camera this time. In fact, I have it right in front of me right now. Oh yeah, Will is coming with us too, so we're gonna have a blast! But I'm gonna miss my Jaime, I hope she recovers by the time I get back. Well have a good weekend everyone!
