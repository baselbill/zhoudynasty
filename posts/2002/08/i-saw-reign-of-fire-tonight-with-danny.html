I Saw Reign Of Fire Tonight With Danny

I saw Reign of Fire tonight with Danny. Not bad of a movie, a lot better than what I had expected. For those of you who haven't heard about it...it's that one movie about how dragons took over the world in 2010 and humans fought back when they were almost extinct. After that we went bowling at Midway, met up with John, Pat, Jaime and Wendy. Pretty fun...but I still can't wait for tomorrow! Woohoo! If we get there in time we'll make it for the pigroast! Here's today's word of the day: fin de siecle (fahn duh sie-kluh) adjective, also fin-de-siecle Defn: Of or pertaining to the end of the nineteenth century, and its climate of sophisticated world-weariness, self-doubt, etc. Poem for today: Toast to Life by Rebecca Ashley Makeup, malls and our high school halls Prom Dates, Boyfriends and College balls Music, computers, classes and lipstick breaks you waited Times at our lockers, the boyfriends we hated 9th grade, 10th, 11th and senior year The night at graduation when we drank the beer College, jobs, marriages and vacations The feeling of sadness, the feeling of elation 5 years, 10 years, 15 and more You've been there for me, but never kept score My dear friend, so pretty and so smart I have always admired you from the very start So here's to 20, 30, 40, 50 or more years That we'll be lifting up our glass and toasting cheers.
