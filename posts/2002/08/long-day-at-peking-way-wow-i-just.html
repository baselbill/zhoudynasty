Long Day At Peking Way Wow I Just

Long day at Peking Way Wow I just finished a 10 hour shift at the restaurant...it's a lot harder than you think. Makes me realize how crappy it is when my parents are there all day for more than a day...that would suck. We were fairly busy today too, people kept coming, it's like they weren't wearing a watch or something. People actually came in at 3:30 in the afternoon to eat lunch! What the hell? Who eats lunch at that time?! Even I don't eat lunch at that time and I work at the restaurant. Anyways, I made a bigass takeout order today as we were about to close. 15 mins before we closed, I got a phone call from someone who said they had a big order. They order a bunch of food and it came out to be like $129.27. I was a little suspicious because I didn't want to receive any phony calls where I make all that food and they don't come pick it up. I made sure they knew our address and I even offered them driving directions. 15 mins later a bunch of girls came to pick up the order. Turns out that they're from Chicago and they're staying at a nearby hotel for tomorrow softball games. It was a good rack in, even made $10 in tips from that one order. My parents always say that I generate a lot of takeout orders whenever I work there, I guess it's true considering one day my mom didn't have 1 single takeout... Sports bikes You know how I wanted to get a sports bike last summer and didn't? Well my friends Arash and Justin beat me to it, they both just got sports bikes. Arash got a Yamaha and Justin got a Suzuki. I hope they don't go and hurt themselves, they've never rode bikes before and neither of them are have liscenses. Well since they both got bikes, that means it's time for me to get mine too...hehe. But it's a little late to get a bike now since this summer is almost over, I should just wait till next summer. Tomorrow I get the day off, but I gotta drive Gordon's dad to Edina so he can play mah-jong with some relatives. That means I'll be hanging around the Edina area for a few hours and getting together with my Mimi.

posted by Billy — Thursday, August 15, 2002 at 10:18 PM
