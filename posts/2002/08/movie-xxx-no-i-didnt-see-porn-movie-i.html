Movie Xxx No I Didnt See Porn Movie

MOVIE: XXX No I didn't see a porn movie, I saw that tripple x movie. Vin Diesel..what a big piece of man. XXX was a pretty good action movie, has lots of amazing stunts that only happens in Hollywood but who cares? It was good. I even caught one of the bad guys using a P90 which is a sub-machine gun that's used in Counter-Strike....hehe. Yes I saw the movie with Steve and Lisa. Poor Lisa thought we were gonna see the movie at the Carmike in Mounds View. She had to drive all the way over here to watch it with us. I felt bad for misleading her so I got her a ticket for the show. What else did I do today? Hmmm....went to the school to buy books but I ended up just buying a large bubble tea from WJ's.
