Shake That Ass Bitch And Let Me See

Shake that ass bitch and let me see what you got... Thursday night we went to the Quest. Yes, the last Thursday before school starts. It was a lot of fun, Danny, Steve and I got there at around 12. Jaime and some of her friends were there too. This time not everyone went, but we got a pretty good dancing group. I brought my LED necklaces there and almost everyone in our group had one, it was pretty cool. Moving to UV Today Steve and I moved our bed and our study table into our apartment. We couldn't assemble them yet because our room was locked by the cleaning people, that sucked. But that's ok cuz Sunday we're all gonna move in and do massive cleaning. China Photos Hey I'm almost done with them. I've got the barebone version up right now. If you people want to see a collection of photos from China, check out these links. - May - June Tomorrow? What am I supposed to do tomorrow? Oh yeah, get ready for school! Damn I'm gonna have to sit here and think of what I need to buy and shit. Doh. Well maybe I'll finish up on the China photos too cuz I don't have much time before school starts and I have to leave my computer. Oh have you guys seen that music video from Dirty Vegas for their song called "Days Go By"? It's a pretty cool video, my new favorite music video excluding any SES videos...hehe.
