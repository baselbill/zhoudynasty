Theres Place For Fun In Your Life

There's a place for fun in your life... Mall of America! Yep, I went there again today. Yes yes I know, I just went there on Saturday with Kenneth. And I know, I'm a bad boyfriend for not seeing Jaime today. But I couldn't get ahold of her and she slept until she had to go to work. =( She should've taken the day off. That's ok, I'm gonna see her tomorrow and bring her some soup to calm her fire. So yeah, the mall was pretty cool today. I went because Pat called me at 12 while I was still sleeping and told me to go there and play Pump cuz he's already there. So I said ok. Well by the time I got dressed, picked up Will and arrived at the mall, it was already 2 and Pat had left, doh! But that's ok, Will and I walked around the mall like 3 times just for fun, I think we went into like 1 store. But he did get 2 shirts at Aeropostal. Well anyways, Danny comes and meets up with us at like 4 and he said he was getting a new wardrobe. He said "what do you think? I need some forest-green shirts." I was like "hell no man, you need something to make you look more clean cut." So from that point on, I became his personal fashion assistant. Yes, we had fun, I gave him a new look. From head to toe, well we still need to shave his head, but that's another day. Today, Danny bought 13 items at the mall! 2 of them were shoes, he got his first pair of leather boots in a long ass time. I was getting sick of seeing him wear those worn-down Newbalance sneakers so I decided to fit him up with a pair of Timberland shoes, they're nice. We also made stops at Old Navy, Gap, and Express for Men. He said "hook me up" and the rest was history. Danny is a new man now, if you see him on the streets, you'll be stunned by his new look, I hope. What's Mall of America without Pump it Up? Yes as you may have guessed, I played Pump. I just can't help it. I first played it when I was waiting for Danny. I tried to play double, it's where you play on both sides. It was pretty hard and I barely passed Funky Tonight. Then I failed on a second song. I'm just not used to it, I felt like I had to do splits on some of the moves. I later played Pump again 10 mins before our movie. This time, I played with a guy because I didn't want to play alone. It was cool because I did good and a bunch of people were behind us watching. Yes, it was really cool. But that Korean kid with the blonde hair was there waiting his turn after me (Yes I know you fear him Pat). I was just glad that I got to go before the blonde hair kid instead of after, he's supposed to be really good. Maybe I'll get as good as him someday and make people like Pat fear my crazy legs. Movie: Blood Work Yes! A murder mystery with Clint Eastwood! I bet most of you haven't heard about this movie and thinks that it sucks. Well I saw it tonight with Will and Danny at Mall of America. The movie was pretty good actually, it had a good plot and like all murder mysteries, very interesting and mysterious. But in the middle of the movie, I guessed who the killer was....and I was right. Yeah for me! I got some FBI skills. What's the movie about? Oh yeah, the movie is about a retired FBI investigator who had a heart transplant. He finds out that the heart he received was from a young woman who was murdered. So the murdered woman's sister asks Eastwood to try and find the killer and so he goes and does that. Interesting huh? Only part in the movie that I didn't like was that Clint Eastwood sleeps with the murdered woman's sister. That's kind of weird cuz the woman had sex with a man who had her sister's heart...? I donno...just kinda weird. Plus there was a like a 7 year old boy in the other room! Do you think Eastwood needs Viagra? I think he used it but Danny doesn't think so. Tomorrow It's gonna be Wednesday, you know what that means....SEAFOOD NIGHT at Mystic!!! Yeah tomorrow I'm gonna go see Jaime, shop at COSTCO, visit Mary's party hopefully and then hit Mystic with Danny boy, anyone want to join us in the attack on the sea? Damn I'm too full to think about that right now cuz I just got back from eating late-night with Danny and Will at Village Wok. It was pretty good, we had beef brisket hot pot, hollow vegetables and bitter melon with beef in black bean sauce. Always that black bean sauce. Yeah yeah yeah, I wrote a lot tonight. But the reason is because I know that people are reading this, they're actually reading this! Can you believe that?! So since they read this, that must mean they're really bored. So I thought I'd just elaborate on my day for them, let them have more to read, to keep them entertained. You know what they ought to do is go to www.thedanceposse.com and register, then chat on there with other people! Yeah the reason I know that they read is because Will and Danny both know that I've seen XXX and that Jaime has a throat infection...yay I have fans!
