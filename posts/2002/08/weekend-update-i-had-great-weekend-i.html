Weekend Update I Had Great Weekend I

Weekend Update I had a great weekend, I don't think I've had this many activities for a while. I'm feeling sore too, I don't know why. Friday: Valley Fair We went to Valley Fair the whole day. It was Jaime, Aries, Steve, Lisa and me. Weather was nice, but got a little couldy later when we decided to go to the water park, I think the only person that was really affected by the temp was Aries, he needs more body fat or something. We went on almost every ride, I got to go on the Power Tower drop for the first time, it was awesome! Oh yeah, Jaime and I played Pump it Up twice and drew some crowds and dropped jaws. After VF After Valley Fair, I saw Signs again with Steve, Lisa, Jaime, Will, Chee, Xai, and Levong. It was funny cuz this time Steve and I knew when the scarry parts will be coming. We turned around to look at Chee, Levong and Xai and got to see them jump out of their seats during those scenes...lol. After the movie me and the guys went to Perkins and celebrated Steve's birthday. Steve also gave Chee the Spongebob that he won at VF for his bday...lol. Saturday: St. Croix River Picnic The picnic was a success! I think everyone had a good time. We had like 20 people show up, it was a lot of people and almost everyone got to try some water sports. You know how I said be by my house by 11:30? Well we operated on Asian time again and didn't get to leave till around 1:30. I hope next time we'll have a better awareness of time. I think a few people on the trip might have a sore ass right now, especially the people who sat in the front of the boat when I drove it over those big waves. After the picnic After the picnic, most of us went to Julie's welcome home/birthday party. It was cool to see everyone there, it felt like the good old days when we used to party at her house and eat good food her parents made. I stayed pretty late till around 2AM with the kareoke group to do some singing and give some people rides in my car. Anyways I got some pictures from the trip and I know other people have them too. I will be posting them up probably within a few days. As for today, I'm gonna be cleaning, washing the car and cleaning the boat. Maybe go to Sam's Club with my mom, she loves Sams.
