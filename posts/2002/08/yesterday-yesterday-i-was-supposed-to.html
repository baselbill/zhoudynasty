Yesterday Yesterday I Was Supposed To

Yesterday Yesterday I was supposed to work at my parents' restaurant but we were so slow that I got off work at 7. Later Jaime and I decided to meet up at the Fuddruckers in Edina. We got a 1 pound burger and a malt to share. It was huge! The burger was good, except it wasn't medium like the way we wanted it. Afterwards we went for a long walk around Centennial Lakes Park. I think most of the time we sat in one of those outdoor wooden swing benches. Around 11 we decided to pay my friend Kenneth a visit since he lives in Edina. Kenneth is lookin' all Japanese now with his dyed hair...I can't get used to it. Anyways, his puppy is real cute and tried to hump Jaime's leg many times. Well I gott work again today, but there's good news. Arash finally got the apartment for us and so now I'm 99% sure I'll be living in University Village for next year!

posted by Billy — Wednesday, August 14, 2002 at 2:36 PM
