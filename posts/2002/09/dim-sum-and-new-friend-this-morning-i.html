Dim Sum And New Friend This Morning I

Dim Sum and a new friend This morning I woke up at 10:30 and went to dim sum with Jaime at 11:30 along with Catherine and some of her friends. We met a guy named David, he just transferred here from Madison and he's studying Graphic Designs. It's weird cuz David lives in UV and when Jaime and I came down in the elevator he was in there with us. David is a cool guy, hope he'll get to hang out and party with us this semester. Oh did I mention that he's also from the same province that I was from? He moved to the US when he was 9. Confession I played CS today. After dim sum Arash called me and told me to go to PCP. I went there and they made me practice with them. I thought okay maybe like 20 mins, but these guys wouldn't let me go. I ended up staying for longer than an hour. We scrimmaged against 3 clans and I lead their team in the first 2 matches. In the 3rd match I just wanted to leave and I didn't even give them any orders to follow, we fell apart and lost horribly. First two matches we won. Eventhough I played so much today, it didn't feel good, it wasn't even fun for me. I guess that's a good thing. Haircut Tonight I cut Steve's hair. He was bugging me about cutting his hair and I didn't want to because I had never done it before. Well tonight I finally did and it turned out pretty nice. I gave him a different style from before and so far Justin and Megan thinks it looks good. Justin didn't believe it was me who cut it. CASA Website I've started to work on the new CASA website. Actually I just made a new logo, the old one kinda sucked. I'm gonna be making the new template soon, very slowly though, I got homework and other crap. If anyone wants to help me, let me know and we can get together. If you don't know anything about making websites but want to learn I can teach you too.
