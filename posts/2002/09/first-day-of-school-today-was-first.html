First Day Of School Today Was First

First Day of School Today was the first day of school. Steve and I both woke up at 8AM for our weight training class in the morning. It's quite nice because we can work out an hour during the class and then another hour right after on our own. Later today I went for my EE discussion and lab and no one showed up. I thought something was suspicious so I checked the class webpage and it said we didn't have any till next week. Saw a lot of familar faces on campus too. I even saw Saba from high school! She went to Toronto the first two years of her college, hopefully she'll get to hang out with us cuz she doesn't know very many people here at the U.
