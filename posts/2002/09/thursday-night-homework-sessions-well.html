Thursday Night Homework Sessions Well

Thursday night homework sessions Well tonight is the night before EE3015 is due. And as usual, we have a bunch of people over doing it together. There's 5 people in our apartment working on these proofs, damn proofs are so damn anal. 53" TV We also got a 53" TV today for the living room. It's HDTV and it's like movie screen shape. I really don't think we need such a beast. It's pretty nice except the new DVD player doesn't seem to show red very well. At least we all thought we were color-blind but I guess it's just the DVD player. Piece of shit. Getting Buff Oh yeah, I'm getting buff, again. I've been consistently doing my mass-gaining workout.
