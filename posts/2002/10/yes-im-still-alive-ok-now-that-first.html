Yes Im Still Alive Ok Now That First

Yes I'm still alive Ok now that the first wave of midterms are over, I'm able to write something down on the blog again. I took 3 midterms in the past week and the results were not bad, better than average. Damn I missed a lot of stuff here, I should've kept up with the blog. Hmmm....let me think of what I did... This weekend, ummmm...CASA had their Game Night, it was kind of lame, people were just sitting around and stuff. I went to downtown Minneapolis this weekend with Lisa and David just to roam around and look for a dance club but we didn't find one. I went to Ryan's house party finally, it was pretty cool. My cousin Jon came up to visit as well. And last night I went to Edina to see the Audi 200 that I'm gonna get soon. It's a 1989 Audi that's an equivalent of a Audi A8 today.
