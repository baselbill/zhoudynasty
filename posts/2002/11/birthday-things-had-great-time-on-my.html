Birthday Things Had Great Time On My

Birthday Things Had a great time on my birthday. For dinner, Jaime, Jimmy, Andrea, Steve, Julie, David and Levong took me to Stewart Anderson's Cattle Company for steak. The steak was awesome and Levong and I had a bottle of wine! Then we went to Tropix with a group of 8 people I think. It was me, Julie, Steve, Lisa, Danny, David, Danny's friend John and Jean. Yeah it was a lot of fun, I even got drunk. People at Trpoix were pretty skanky though. CAAM Dance Went to see CAAM's annual performance this weekend on Saturday. It was really cool to see all the old CAAM people and to see Pat dancing in the production. Some parts of it was kind of boring, I fell asleep. After we got back from that show, we stopped by Dinnaken where Andrea and Jimmy and threw me a surprise party! But it was too bad that they were too drunk themselves...hehe they didn't even get me drunk. Anyways, my laptop is supposed to arrive today. I hope it's here by the time I get back from class!
