Sweaty Quest So Last Night Was

Sweaty Quest So last night was Thursday night...naturally we flocked to the Quest because of the Asian power that can be felt all across the Twin Cities. There were a lot of people there, but unfortunately there were more guys than girls. According to Andrea and Jessica, the guys there were more thuggish this time, but then again those girls weren't drunk. Overall it was fun but I think I sweated too much. In the end my shirt was soaked and people were afraid to touch me cuz I was a sponge.
