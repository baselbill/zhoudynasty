Non Stop Partying Wow I Think I Am

Non-stop Partying Wow I think I am almost partied out. I say almost because today is David's birthday and it's going to be the last party for me for a while. All this excitement does no good for me and my wallet. Thursday night was Tasha's birthday party at Townsquare Garden. It was a pretty good party but I think it could've been better if the bar had more drinks and if her party didn't have to compete with the Quest that night. Overall it was a lot of fun and I randomly met another girl that already knew my dad before she knew me. My dad was also her instructor at CADT. Friday night we went out to Harvey's for Saba's birthday last week. Originally we were going to go to Ocean Club after 10 because that's when Harveys ends their happy hour. But by that time everyone was having a lot of fun and decided to stay at Harvey's. Kenneth came too, finally, after years of begging him to hang out and party with me. Last night Linh threw a big asian party at her house because her parents were out of town. I kept myself under control because the past two nights were already a lot for me. However other people were pretty out there, dancing and getting drunk. Great party but too bad she had to spend all day today cleaning up. Today is a good day. All I've done today was lay on my deck and absorbed sun. And thought about things. Sigh, I wish I could see my future more clearly.
