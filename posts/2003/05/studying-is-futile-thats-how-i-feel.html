Studying Is Futile Thats How I Feel

Studying is futile That's how I feel like for one of my EE classes. Damnit all this studying for this class is not helping, I feel like I'm trying to read a big fat book of Hebrew or something. So yeah...I haven't updated in awhile. I put up some pictures from the CASA boat dance but those are only my own, I will be adding David and Jimmy's when I get the time. The boat dance was great, I loved it. I got to bond with my single guys and also have a little fun with the ladies. =) So yeah check out those pics. Oh yeah we had that CASA picnic too! That was cool, I don't know if I already talked about it here on the site but a big water fight broke out. All started when CASA board members loaded up water guns and water balloons then ambushed the unsuspecting members...good times. I think I got pics for that too. And my dad is back from China. Don't worry he doesn't have SARS. He quarantined himself for 10 days cuz that's the incubation period or something. So it's all good...come say hi to TJ.
