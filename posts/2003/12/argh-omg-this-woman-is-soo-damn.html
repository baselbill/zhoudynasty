Argh Omg This Woman Is Soo Damn

ARGH! OMG this woman is soo damn annoying!!! Please people, when you call a tech helpline, have some patience. If you don't have the patience yourself, how can you expect others to have the patience to deal with your ass. God damnit this lady just refuses to follow my exact directions, saying she can't find the option when it is staring at her in the face!
