It Nerd Walkin Down Street

IT nerd, walkin' down the street... IT nerd, the kind you dont want to meet... Why do my majors suck so damn bad? I don't understand. I thought I had everything planned out in high school when I wanted to become a computer engineer. This is the time of the year where I feel like I'm on the edge of breaking down, especially when your classes all have assignments and projects due on the same day. How's an IT student supposed to live his life? I know mine is depressing. I took my DSP midterm today, the class where graduate students and fobs from China dominate. The exam is open book open notes. So I printed out all the homework solutions that has to do with this exam and even homework solutions from other universities studying the same topic. What do the grads and fobs do? They come into the test with 2 or 3 other text books that have to do with DSP. It was disgusting. They're already smarter and more knowledgeable in this subject and they need to make the curve even higher. Needless to say, I felt the ravaging throughout the test. Afterwards, Jimmy and I immediately headed to the computer lab to check on our stocks as that is probably our only hope of success in the future. So time is winding down and what do I have to do? A BUNCH OF SHIT! Why am I blogging you ask? I feel like I need to vent, there's just too much inside of me. Yeah I think I need some sleep now, but first I must go pinch those nipples of David. (Helps me to relieve stress ^_^)
