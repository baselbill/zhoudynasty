Crap Software Omg Ive Spent Past 4

Crap software Omg I've spent the past 4 hours trying to make a stupid document with pictures and boxes. It is ridiculous how unflexible Microsoft Word is. I made an object box go behind the text and then I couldn't select that object box anymore! wtf! After several tries and looking through their stupid help files I couldn't find anyway to select that object again. It just sits there, can't do anything with it now. And I couldn't undo because Word crashed and auto saved right when I made the object go in the background. So I said screw that there must be a better program out there. So I go and download Adobe PageMaker. What a joke of a program. The format and efficiency of this program reminds me of Windows 3.1! There is an undo button this time, but 75% of the time you can't use it!!!! And if you could use it, you can only undo once. I accidently deleted a large part of my text that took me 20 mins to resize and place around the pictures, and guess what, I can't f*#king undo it!!! Work is stressing me out now. I think I will go work on my pen spinning techniques.
