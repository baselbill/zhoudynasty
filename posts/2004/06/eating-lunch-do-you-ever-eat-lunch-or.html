Eating Lunch Do You Ever Eat Lunch Or

Eating lunch Do you ever eat lunch or dinner by yourself? For some reason I can't go out and eat by myself. I've always had this problem ever since I can remember. Anyways, today for lunch I was going to go get a sub to bring back. But I found a little Chinese restaurant called Lucky Inn. I knew it had a buffet and it was a family style restaurant and I knew that I'm tired of eating those buffet things, but for some reason I couldn't help myself and I went to eat there. The food wasn't bad and the price is ok, but the buffet is small compared to my family's buffet. The whole time I was eating I was wondering about who owns this place and whether or not I know him/her since everyone knows each other here in MN. So does your family own Luck Inn in Eden Prairie?
