Bacon Owns Me Wow I Love Bacon

Bacon owns me Wow I love bacon. They have bacon at work for breakfast in the morning. Every morning I go down to the cafeteria to look at the thick cut yummy bacon but I don't buy it because it costs $0.45 a piece!!!!! So expensive! But this morning I was feeling good and its Friday so when I went down there this time, I actually bought some. I got 3 slices of bacon and the lunch lady thought that it was only 2 because 2 slices stuck together. So she only charged $0.90 instead $1.35, SCORE!! Yeah the bacon was good, I brought it back to my cube and ate it secretly or my co-workers would be drooling all over the place.
