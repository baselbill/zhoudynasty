School In Session School Has Started

School in session School has started and things are busy already. Busy trying to reorient myself and think like a student. I feel like some old guy back in school, it feels not right. But the other day I saw a really old guy, probably in his 80s, walking around campus with a backpack on. He looked like a college student and made me feel less old. The class I feel the most awkward in is my Chem1021 class. It's got over a hundred people in lecture and most are freshmen. The first day of class some girl was being very friendly and had the freshman mentality of meeting people, asked me which dorm I lived in. When I told her that I was a senior and lived in an apartment, they looked at me like I'm some kind of a freak show. Then someone in lecture raised their hand and asked if the professor would like them to highlight and underline in the book as they read... My new apartment is pretty nice, quite a bit smaller than Dinnaken though. David and I sharing a room again and then David's white friend Mike has his own room. I finally got my internet connection to work. I've got free internet through someone's wireless router..hehe. I'm going fishing in a bit with Danny. I figure the first week of lectures isn't that big and plus I'll be reading the book on the fish boat.
