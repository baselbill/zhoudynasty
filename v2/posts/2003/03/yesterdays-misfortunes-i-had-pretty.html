Yesterdays Misfortunes I Had Pretty

Yesterday's Misfortunes I had a pretty rough day yesterday. All because of the lack of sleep and little bad luck. Started with me trying to make ramen in the morning. I put a cup of ramen in our microwave without water. It was until 2 minutes later that I smelled something funny. I turned around and there was thick smoke just pouring out of my microwave. I opened up the microwave to get of that ramen but that only released the smoke into the entire living room. I ended up opening my windows and turning on all the fans. I even had to stand under the fire alarm to wave at the smoke so it doesn't set it off. That was rough, and I ended up smelling like burnt ramen the whole the day. Then I went to class and I thought maybe I'll take a little nap because I felt so damn tired and couldn't concentrate. So I slept for like 5 mins and I woke up to my professor saying "You know it's funny how it's the same people who sleep ever day in lecture. I would prefer just to stay home and sleep, it's more comfortable." And he was looking at me! Other people in the class started looking at me and the TA even laughed. That was so wrong because it was the first time I ever fell asleep in there. I think he's asian-blind and mistaken me for Steve. Steve slept through the whole thing and didn't even hear the prof make fun of me. Old School Oh my god I haven't seen a movie this funny for a while. Old School with Will Ferrell is hillarious. It reminds me of Officespace but a lot crazier cuz Will is in it. Anyways you guys have to see this movie as soon as you can. Refugees from the Cold So we went out to downtown again last night. It was me, Dave, John Kim, Andrea, Lisa and Megan. We were going to go bar hopping. But we didn't hop cuz it was too cold. We ran from our parking lot to the Refuge cuz it was the closest bar with people in it. It was a lot more fun this time cuz our group was bigger and it wasn't as damn crowded. They played a lot of great songs, songs that I would play if I was the DJ.
