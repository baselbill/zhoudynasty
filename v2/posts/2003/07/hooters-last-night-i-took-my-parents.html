Hooters Last Night I Took My Parents

Hooters Last night I took my parents to Hooters for their happy hour. They were amazed that I can eat so many wings. Oh we tried the raw oysters, oyster roast, and steam clams too. Here's my review. Raw Oysters: 10/10 They are absolutely awesome! You think the raw oysters at the Mystic Lake seafood buffet is good? Well these oysters put them to shame! Put on your bib Khuong cuz when you get these oysters, they're fresh and juicy. Not a hint of deadness. Their oysters actually taste like it comes from the ocean. They're served with the cocktail sauce, melted butter, horse raddish, lemon wedges and crackers. I don't know why crackers, maybe I don't know the art of eating raw oysters. They were $9.95 for 12. A little pricy, but well worth every penny if ya like em oysters fresh and clean! Oyster Roast: 7/10 They come in a bucket. And they're fresh too! They're a lot of fun to eat cuz the Hooters girl gives you a towl and this tool for shucking them. You have to open the oysters yourself like a fisherman. The only drawback with these is that they come all shrivelled up cuz they're cooked. But it's a lot of fun to shuck them. $11.95 for 18. Steam Clams: 6/10 Well I'm a little bias but if you ask me, they would taste better raw! These clams weren't special, they weren't cooked in a sauce or anything, just steamed. They did taste a little spicy. $9.95 for 13. Hope that was helpful to you seafood lovers out there. Hey if you're heading out next time for Hooters wings and oysters, gimme a call! Picture Day It was a nice day outside today so I decided to take some pictures on my motorcycle.
