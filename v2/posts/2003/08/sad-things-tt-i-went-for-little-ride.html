Sad Things Tt I Went For Little Ride

Sad things T_T I went for a little ride yesterday with Lisa. Everything was fine and we were cruising at slow speeds. Then we got to an enclosed road and decided to trade bikes and try each other's ouch. Well I got on hers and went about 30 feet, then I hear CRACK! I turned around and she tipped my bike over! The bike wasn't even moving yet and she tried to balance it but couldn't and the whole thing fell over. It got scratched up some more. Well at least I can go buy that touch-up paint now. -_- Last night my friend Sara came to visit my new apartment and we decided to go grab a bite to eat. We hopped on our motorcycles and decided to take the long way there, go ride around campus first. We got excited and started riding a little faster. But then when we got to this one bridge I said "We gotta becareful cuz there are a lot of cops on this bridge." And we still raced over that thing. I guess she just couldn't let me go faster than her. I started to do a lean and that's when I noticed the flashing lights. F(*@&#*(@&#(@#*&(@&! We got pulled over, Sara was terrified cuz she had a reckless driving citation last month and was sure she's not gonna be able to ride her bike anymore after this one. The cop was nice and said he's gonna give us SPEED ON BRIDGE violations instead of reckless driving...So what does that mean?
