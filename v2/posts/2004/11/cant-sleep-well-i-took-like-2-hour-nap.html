Cant Sleep Well I Took Like 2 Hour

Can't sleep Well I took like a 2 hour nap today and now I can't sleep. It's almost 4:30am, why not write something huh? Have you ever taken one of those exams that was just impossible? I'm talkin about one of those classes where you don't attend for a month, "compare" homework with friends, and have no clue what the prof is trying to say and then all of a sudden there's an exam. The exam comes and you're confused as to what to study because the prof can't really speak english and you haven't ever attended class. Then you go ahead and cram like usual except only to find out that there is wayyyy too much to cram and everything you didn't learn last month relates to this exam. So then you just give up and take the exam just so you don't get an incomplete in the class. Yeah, ever had one of those? Well I did last week, god it felt horrible. This is one of the worst cases of procrastination I have endured, ever. It got so bad that instead of cramming in those last 2 hours, I just gave up and took a nap on the couch while my friends continued. Then yesterday, my friends asked me to check the website and see what my score was. We were all joking around that I probably got the lowest score in the class, maybe a 23... To all our astonishment, I got a 74, 3 points above average. I did better than most of the people whom I "studied" with and they actually knew their stuff. Incredible, how can this be?!?! No time to figure that out, we head to Hooters happy hour and eat chicken wings and drink beer to celebrate. Come to think of it, I've had an unusual string of luck this semester when it comes to exams and quizes. I've managed to stay above average in all the classes that I don't attend or every study for. The ONLY class I am struggling in is Chemistry, the first one, the one with all the freshmen. I go to lecture, I do the homework and I study. And I'm getting my ass kicked in the class!!! So I've skipped all the lectures last week and hopefully this week to try and improve my score. If this actually works, I'm gonna have to write a book on how being a major slacker actually improves your grade. Now I've gotta go to bed, so I can make it to my Chem lab. Too bad attendance is required in this one, or I'd skip it too. :P
